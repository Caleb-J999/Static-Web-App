# Static-Web-App
Designed to practice building prototype pages. 

**Overview:**
    The theme of this web-app is Pokémon. It includes images of randomly selected Pokémon each representing various Pokémon types. Under each image, there is a short description of the Pokémon. The web-app also includes which generation that the Pokémon was first introduced, as well as which region, (geographical location), that the Pokémon is found in within the games / animated series. 

**What is Pokémon:**
    Pokémon is a fictional universe full of collectible, magical creatures with unique powers that humans catch, train, and pit against each other in friendly sports combat.

**What is a Pokémon type:**
    Pokémon types are core elemental or thematic classifications assigned to Pokémon and their Pokémon moves that dictates their strengths and weaknesses in Pokémon battles (water type, grass type, fire type, etc.). Each Pokémon possess up to two types, but the Pokémon included in this web-app will include one type each. 

**What are Pokémon regions / generations:** 
    Pokémon regions are the fictional geographical settings where the games and animated series take place, while generations are the eras of the franchise that group new games, new gameplay mechanics, and new batches of Pokémon together. With each new generation comes a new region, currently there are 9 regions and 9 generations.
