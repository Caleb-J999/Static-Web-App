Data & Attribution

The characters featured in this gallery are Pokémon from the Pokémon franchise. All character descriptions and abilities are based on their appearances in these officially licensed Pokémon productions. Images are sourced from public stock photo services for demonstration purposes.

I chose this theme because I have been a huge fan of the Pokémon franchise since I was little and I wanted to create a space where others could appreciate the art and design of these beloved creatures.
